01 — About Frankie

Opera, musical theatre & a classical repertoire.

Frankie is a versatile vocalist and performer specializing in opera, musical theatre, with a classical repertoire.

An upcoming graduate of the University of Central Florida (UCF) with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Musical Theatre Performance, she moves easily between the opera stage and the musical theatre stage, and she’s fluent in Spanish.

Available for live performances, singing gigs, and private voice lessons. Contact for bookings and inquiries.

05 — Teaching

Private voice lessons

Frankie is an enthusiastic and patient voice teacher who loves working with students of all ages and skill levels.

She has served as a teaching assistant for CFCArts’ “Upbeat!” Program and provided private voice lessons to children at The Christ School in Orlando.

She is also fluent in Spanish! Lessons available in English or Español.
